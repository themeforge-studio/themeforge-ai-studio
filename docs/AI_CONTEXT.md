# AI Context Document

## Version 1.0

---

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

ThemeForge AI Studio is a private AI-powered platform designed to help a single creator produce, organize, optimize and commercialize digital assets for Android and iPhone ecosystems.

Primary outputs:

* Wallpapers
* Icon Packs
* Widgets
* Marketing Assets
* Future Interactive Themes

The platform is intended to dramatically reduce production time while increasing commercial output.

---

# PRIMARY GOAL

Reduce by at least 70% the time required to create and prepare digital products for distribution on:

* Google Play Store
* Apple App Store

---

# TARGET USER

Single creator.

Not SaaS.

Not multi-tenant.

Not marketplace.

Single-owner productivity platform.
